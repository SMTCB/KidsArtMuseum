# Roadmap

**The single canonical roadmap.** It replaces two documents that contradicted each other:
`ROADMAP-static-phase.md` (a family museum that might grow) and `product_roadmap-2026-05-29.txt`
(a funded startup on a Q3-2026 migration clock). Both are archived with tombstones. Neither
is current. Do not plan from them.

> ⚠️ **This roadmap is conditional on [ADR-0004](../decisions/0004-product-ambition.md),
> **Updated 2026-08-22:** ADR-0004 is decided — **venture-scale SaaS**. Horizon 1 and 2 are
> unchanged by that decision and remain the plan. Horizon 3 no longer forks, but it also
> cannot be scheduled yet: the strategy behind the decision is not written down (STR-05).

## Where we actually are — 2026-08-22

Verified against the running site, not against documents.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Artworks** | 11, by 3 artists (Miguel 6–7, Manuel 7, Muna 4) |
| **Wall themes** | 2 — Cubo Branco, Parede de Galeria |
| **Animation routines** | 11 bespoke, plus 33 Sala de Experimentação effects — 44 keys in `window.ANIMS` |
| **Health** | No console errors. Every `anim` resolves; every `HALLWAY_ORDER` id exists; all assets present. |
| **Hosting** | Vercel, auto-deploy from `main`. ~$0/month. |
| **Intake** | `intake/` tray + Claude Code. Works, but has never run its archival step (BACKLOG FND-08). |
| **Known weak point** | 25 MB of images on the hallway (BACKLOG QUA-01) |

The last three months went into the Sala de Experimentação — 33 visual effects. Read as a
signal about ambition this looked like a craft investment rather than a scaling one; that
reading was wrong. Per ADR-0004, **the site was built as a deliberate experiment with an
audience of two**, to learn what the product should feel like before committing to build it.
The creative layer is the experiment's output, and it is the most valuable asset here.

## Horizon 1 — Make what exists excellent · Sprints 0–3, ~Aug–Oct 2026

**Holds under every ADR-0004 outcome.** Nothing here is wasted work.

The museum is real and it works. It is not yet fast, not yet shareable one piece at a time,
and it does not yet carry the children's voices. Those three gaps are the whole horizon.

- **Foundation** — track the docs, rotate the leaked token, remove the stale bundle, make the
  operating docs true again *(FND-01…08)*
- **Performance** — thumbnails and `srcset`, production React, a smoke test *(QUA-01…05)*
- **Shareable** — stable `/obra/<id>` URLs, print view *(P1-02, P1-03)*
- **Frictionless intake** — HEIC conversion *(P1-01)*
- **Character** — third wall theme, animated intro, better theme names *(P1-04…06)*
- **Voice** — audio discovery dots, pending [ADR-0007](../decisions/0007-audio-discovery-dots.md) *(AUD-01…02)*

**Success:** a grandparent opens a link on a phone, it loads in under two seconds, and they
hear the child explain the drawing.

## Horizon 2 — Remove the developer from the loop · ~Nov 2026–Jan 2027

Adding art currently requires a laptop, a terminal, and Claude Code. That is fine for one
technical parent and fatal for anyone else — **including this family on a Sunday evening.**
Self-serve upload is worth building even if the product never leaves the family.

- Upload UI, replacing the `intake/` tray *(P2-01…07)*
- Serverless `/api/add-artwork`, model per [ADR-0005](../decisions/0005-vision-model-provider.md)
- Approval behaviour per [ADR-0009](../decisions/0009-approval-step-in-automated-upload.md)
- Artwork editing — required under every ADR-0009 option *(P2-06)*
- In-browser audio recording, if [ADR-0007](../decisions/0007-audio-discovery-dots.md) went that way *(AUD-03)*

Realistically **two sprints**, not the four days the archived roadmap estimated. That
estimate predated the approval, editing, and error-state work.

**Success:** a piece goes from phone camera to gallery wall in under three minutes, with no
terminal.

## Horizon 3 — The SaaS · not yet schedulable

ADR-0004 settled the destination on 2026-08-22: this becomes a product other families pay
for. What it did **not** settle is the route — target market, wedge, pricing, and
go-to-market. None of that is written down in this repo.

**This horizon is deliberately left unplanned rather than filled with a plausible-looking
plan.** The material to build one from does exist — `docs/archive/product_roadmap-2026-05-29.txt`
sequences Supabase and auth *(ADR-0006)*, Stripe and tiers, the Escola pilot, and
print-on-demand; `BUSINESS_OVERVIEW.md` sketches the commercial shape; `proj_eval` names the
distribution risk. But copying that sequence forward would dress up an assumption as a
schedule. It was written in May against a funding scenario that did not happen, and its
60-day clock already lapsed once without anyone noticing.

**The gate is STR-05: write the strategy down.** Two things then follow quickly, and neither
can start before it:

| | Why it waits |
|---|---|
| Horizon 3 sequencing | The order of Supabase → billing → Escola → POD depends entirely on who the first paying customer is, which is a positioning question |
| **STR-06 — visual identity** | Confirmed on 2026-08-22: none exists. No Claude Design project, no `docs/brand/`, no exported assets. `SCREENS.md` is the brief written for this work in July and never used. An identity commissioned before positioning is decoration. |

What is safe to say now: budget 4–8 weeks full-time for the platform rebuild before the
first paying customer, treat the current codebase as a prototype to harvest rather than
extend, and take the distribution risk in `proj_eval` seriously — a $49/yr B2C product
cannot absorb paid acquisition.

`docs/product/SCREENS.md` inventories all 56 screens. Eight exist today.

## Principles that do not change

Carried forward from the archived roadmap. These survived because they are about the
product's character, not its phase.

1. **The painting is the painting.** No auto-enhance, no filter, no AI manipulation of the artwork.
2. **One animation idea per piece.** Complexity is the enemy of delight.
3. **Portuguese first** — [ADR-0002](../decisions/0002-portuguese-only-ui.md).
4. **The child is the artist.** The UI celebrates the child's voice, not the technology.
5. **Static by default** — [ADR-0001](../decisions/0001-static-first-architecture.md). Complexity has a cost.
