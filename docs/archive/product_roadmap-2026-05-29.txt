FMB · ART: Strategic Product Roadmap

Timeline: Q3 2026 – Q2 2027

This roadmap focuses on transitioning from a static MVP to a scalable SaaS platform, establishing viral growth loops, and unlocking high-margin physical product revenue.

Phase 1: Foundation & The Growth Loop (Q3 2026)

Goal: Move off the static architecture, enable self-serve onboarding, and establish the viral referral mechanic.

Features

Self-Serve Upload UI: Replace the GitHub/Claude Code workflow. Parents upload directly via a web interface.

Audio "Discovery Dots": Allow parents to record 10-15 second audio clips of the child explaining the artwork, attached to specific hotspots on the painting. (This is the emotional "killer feature").

The "Share to Grandparents" Loop: Optimized sharing flow. When visitors view a museum link, prominently feature a "Start your own child's museum" CTA.

Watermarked Free Tier: Free tier exports (images/videos of the animated art) include a subtle FMB · ART watermark to drive organic social acquisition.

Technical Architecture

Database Migration: Implement Supabase (Postgres) to manage user accounts, family relationships, and artwork metadata.

Backend Overhaul: Move to a Next.js App Router architecture deployed on Vercel.

API Integration: Connect the frontend directly to Anthropic's Claude Vision API via secure serverless functions for real-time artwork analysis and JSON generation upon upload.

Storage: Migrate image assets from the GitHub repo to Supabase Storage.

Format Handling: Implement server-side HEIC-to-JPEG conversion (crucial for iPhone users).

Physical Products

No physical products in Phase 1. Focus entirely on digital retention and platform stability.

Phase 2: Monetization & B2B Pilot (Q4 2026)

Goal: Launch the paid subscription tiers, introduce physical gifting ahead of the holidays, and pilot the "Escola" tier.

Features

Subscription Management: Integrate Stripe for handling the "Família" ($49/yr) and "Escola" ($149/yr) subscriptions.

Expanded Themes: Release 3-5 new premium "Wall Themes" exclusive to the paid tiers.

Escola Pilot (Classroom Mode): Roll out features for teachers: bulk uploads, student tagging, and a public "Classroom Gallery" view. Pilot this with 5-10 early childhood educators.

"Year in Review" Digital Export: An automated, highly shareable video summarizing the child's artistic progress over the year.

Technical Architecture

Payment Gateway: Stripe webhook integration and customer portal implementation.

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Implement strict permissions in Supabase to handle complex relationships in the Escola tier (Teacher > Class > Student > Parent).

Video Generation (R&D): Begin experimenting with lightweight server-side video generation (e.g., using Remotion) for the "Year in Review" feature.

Physical Products

The "Museum Pass" Gift Card: A beautifully designed digital (and potentially printable) gift code that grandparents can purchase to gift a 1-year Família subscription.

NFC "Smart Frame" Prototype: Begin sourcing partnerships for a premium physical frame that includes an NFC chip. Tapping a phone to the frame instantly opens the child's digital museum.

Phase 3: Physical Revenue & Expansion (Q1 - Q2 2027)

Goal: Launch high-margin physical products to maximize LTV and scale B2B acquisition through schools.

Features

One-Click Print Store: Native integration within the app allowing parents to order physical items directly from their digital gallery.

Escola Tier General Release: Launch the classroom product publicly, using the resulting parent invitations as a B2B2C acquisition channel.

Collaborative Museums: Allow multiple families (e.g., cousins) to contribute to a shared "Grandparents Museum."

Technical Architecture

Print-on-Demand (POD) API: Integrate with a global POD partner (e.g., Printful, Gelato) to automate physical order fulfillment and shipping directly from the app.

Advanced Analytics: Implement product analytics (e.g., PostHog) to deeply understand user drop-off during the upload flow and conversion rates from shared links.

Physical Products

The Annual "Museum Book": A high-quality, hardcover, automated photobook containing the year's artwork alongside the AI-generated titles and descriptions. ($80-$120 price point).

"Masterpiece Prints": Individual, museum-quality giclée prints of specific artworks, shipped with an NFC-enabled title card linking to the digital animation.
